Frequently asked questions

How do I buy a ticket?

Find an event on the Discover page, open its details, select your ticket type and quantity, then confirm the purchase with your Pass Spot wallet balance.

How do I fund my wallet?

Go to Wallet → Deposit. Enter an amount, choose your payment method, and confirm. In this prototype, deposits are simulated.

How do I withdraw earnings?

Organizers can go to Withdrawals, select their bank, enter the amount (up to the available balance), and submit a request.

Is my payment secure?

In the production version, all payments are processed by licensed Nigerian payment providers with bank-grade encryption. This frontend prototype does not process real payments.
