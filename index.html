Frequently asked questions

Your ticket is delivered instantly as a QR-coded digital pass in the My Tickets section of your Pass Spot account. No printing required — simply show your phone at the gate.
Refund policies are set by each organizer. If an event allows refunds, you'll see a "Request refund" option on your ticket. For cancellations by the organizer, you're always refunded in full.
Sign up for an Organizer account, then use the Create Event page to publish your event in minutes. You can define multiple ticket tiers, track sales in real time and manage attendees from a single dashboard.
Pass Spot supports all major Nigerian debit and credit cards, bank transfers and USSD. Payment methods shown during checkout are always secure and PCI-compliant.
